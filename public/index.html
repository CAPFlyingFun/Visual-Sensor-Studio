    Photo

    The live stream stays responsive; the shutter briefly asks the SAME camera track for its maximum mode, waits for a real decoded frame at the new size, renders the ACTIVE filter once at that measured size, saves the JPEG, then restores the responsive stream. A short pause during capture is the trade. Saved dimensions are what the camera actually granted — never the request, never upscaled.

    Recording

    RGB records the camera stream directly while the stream fits the encoder's frame limit; above it, and for every filter, the clip is the same GPU render the preview shows, at the RECORD IN size. The container is the browser's own choice, and every number below is measured from the finished file — never from what was asked.

    Encoder envelope probe. Records a synthetic moving-noise canvas through the same recorder at 4:3 sizes bracketing the H.264 Level 5.2 frame limit (36,864 macroblocks), then MAX at 30 / 15 / 10 / 5 fed fps — each file decoded. About 30 s; nothing is saved. Its verdict becomes this device's ENCODER capability. 

    
        
    

        
        
    
          
    Night / GPU precision
    
          
    
            For each candidate accumulator format: allocate a texture, attach it to a
            framebuffer, check completeness, render known fractional values, read them back
            through an amplifying pass, and verify they survived. RGBA8 is probed as a
            CONTROL that is expected to FAIL — a test that passes everything proves
            nothing. The last row tries a full Night-sized ping-pong pair. It runs on its own
            throwaway context, so a loss cannot take the camera down; nothing is saved and
            Night is not changed.

    What this build is
    
          
    
            V2 Milestones A–C: the camera shell, the frame-geometry authority, the GPU
            render contract and recording truth. The live stream is a performance decision;
            the photo is a quality decision (the camera's maximum, fetched only for the shot);
            a clip is measured from its own file and held under the encoder's measured frame
            limit. Preview, photo and filtered recording are ONE fragment shader at explicit
            sizes, and the truth table above is the authority's live output — every row
            a separate fact with its own policy.
          
    
          
    
            Milestone D has begun: Motion is the first temporal tool, its frame history held
            at ANALYSIS resolution and saying so. The lens workbench (E) follows. The legacy
            app is unaffected and remains the default URL.
