The benchmark applies each rate to the live track and counts presented frames. It never calls getUserMedia, so it cannot re-prompt for permission or drop the preview. Effective detail samples a centre crop at 1:1 pixel scale and reports whether those pixels carry real information or interpolation — a track can report 4K truthfully while a scaler stretches a smaller sensor mode to fill it. Point the camera at something textured; a blank wall has no detail to measure.
