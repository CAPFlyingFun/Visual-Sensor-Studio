Photo

The live stream stays responsive; the shutter briefly asks the SAME camera track for its maximum mode, waits for a real decoded frame at the new size, renders the ACTIVE filter once at that measured size, saves the JPEG, then restores the responsive stream. A short pause during capture is the trade. Saved dimensions are what the camera actually granted — never the request, never upscaled.

Recording

RGB records the camera stream directly — no render in the path, the stream's own size. A filter records the same GPU render the preview shows, at the RECORD IN size. The container is the browser's own choice, and the ENCODED row is measured from the finished file — never from what was asked.

What this build is

V2 Milestones A–C: the camera shell, the frame-geometry authority, the GPU render contract and recording truth. The live stream is a performance decision; the photo is a quality decision (the camera's maximum, fetched only for the shot); a clip is measured from its own file. Preview, photo and filtered recording are ONE fragment shader at explicit sizes, and the truth table above is the authority's live output — every row a separate fact with its own policy.

Temporal tools (D) and the lens workbench (E) come next. The legacy app is unaffected and remains the default URL.
